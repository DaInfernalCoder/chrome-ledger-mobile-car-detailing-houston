# Chrome Ledger design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want the vehicle inspected in useful light instead of hidden under heavy gloss effects. The page should make service scope understandable and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Ledger ink — `#17242B`
- Chrome shadow — `#203943`
- Record gold — `#F0B94C`
- Verified mint — `#7BC7B7`
- Aluminum — `#E3E8E5`
- Daylight — `#FAFBF8`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

Reuse the precision/finish frame as an inspection ledger that records cabin, wheels, glass, and paint as separate visible checks.

## Signature

A measured ledger grid reveals four inspection zones and keeps service scope readable at a glance.

## Critique

The first concept leaned toward decorative bookkeeping. The revision keeps only useful ledger lines and inspection marks tied to real detailing work.
